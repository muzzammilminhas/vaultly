Built Vaultly, a privacy-first document vault for Android — and this time I mean privacy literally: no cloud, no account, no network calls of any kind. Everything happens on the device.

Photograph or import a document (an ID, a receipt, a handwritten note) and Vaultly runs on-device OCR to pull out the text, encrypts the image with AES before it ever touches disk, and indexes the extracted text in a local SQLite FTS5 table for instant full-text search — not a substring filter, an actual ranked search index. A PIN/biometric lock guards the app on launch and whenever it comes back from the background.

The three things I actually cared about proving to myself while building this:
- On-device OCR (Google ML Kit) that never leaves the phone
- Real full-text search over encrypted content, ranked and searchable as you type
- AES encryption at rest that I verified rather than assumed — I pulled the raw file off the device mid-build and confirmed it doesn't decode as an image without going through the app

Flutter + Riverpod, sqflite/FTS5, flutter_secure_storage backed by the Android Keystore, local_auth. Built in phases with a real commit history, from an empty folder to a signed release APK.
